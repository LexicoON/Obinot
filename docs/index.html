How it works

From voice to notes in three steps.

01

Record

Hit record and talk — or import an audio file you already have. It keeps recording in the background with the screen off.

02

Read

AI transcribes and structures it into a tidy Markdown note: headings, lists, tables, formulas and diagrams. Tidy it up, summarize it or analyze it.

03

Ask

Chat with any note. Ask follow-ups or have a passage explained. The conversation is saved with the note itself.

Features

Everything a voice note needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Notes that read like notes

A custom Markdown reader with tables, code, checklists you can tick, native math and Mermaid diagrams that render offline. Long-press any formula to copy its LaTeX.

Capture, your way

Fast mode transcribes on-device; Accurate mode sends the full audio to AI. Live waveform, background recording and import for any audio you already have.

Your keys, your providers

Use your own Gemini or Groq key, or let Dynamic mode pick the best one per task and stretch free quotas. Rotate several keys when one hits its limit.

  • Groq
  • Gemini
  • Dynamic
Get a free key

Find anything, instantly

Full-text search across every note, colored labels, pins and a trash you can recover from. Organized without effort.

Edit, format, share

Rewrite the summary in Markdown with a live preview, switch the reading font, find a word, replace the audio, copy or export — all from one sheet.

Speaks your language

The app comes in English, Spanish and German, and the AI answers in 23 languages.

Local-first

Notes, audio and settings live on your device. No account, no telemetry. AI requests go straight from your phone to your provider.

  • No account
  • No telemetry
  • Stored on-device
  • Your own API key
  • Open source

Backup & export

Full backups, single-note files and Markdown export. Binot 1.x backups import too.

Look & feel

Light or dark. Your call.

Material 3 Expressive with springy buttons, a wavy audio progress bar and adaptive layouts for phones and tablets. Pick Auto, Light, Dark or AMOLED, and one of 7 Material You color palettes (tonal spot, vibrant, expressive, fruit salad, neutral, fidelity and monochrome).

Get started

Up and running in five minutes.

Obinot is free. The AI part runs on a free key from the provider you choose — we'll walk you through it.

  1. 1

    Install the APK

    Download it from GitHub and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser once.

  2. 2

    Add a free API key

    Pick Groq, Gemini or both. Creating a key takes about two minutes.

  3. 3

    Press record

    Talk. Open the note. That's it.

GroqRecommended for its blazing fast speed.
GeminiBest for very long audio recordings (not recommended for free API).
DynamicBoth working together, picking the best for each task.

    Coming from Binot 1.x?

    Bring your notes along.

    Obinot installs as a separate app, so both can live on your phone. Your data moves with you — backups work both ways.

    1. In Binot 1.x: Settings → Data & System → Backup
    2. Save the .binotbak file
    3. In Obinot: Settings → Data & System → Import
    4. Done: notes, audio, labels and preferences come over. API keys don't, so add them again once.
