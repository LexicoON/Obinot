FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about privacy, AI, installing and how Obinot works.

Is Obinot free?

Yes. The app is free and open source. The AI features use an API key from Groq or Gemini; both have free tiers. If you go past those, billing is between you and the provider.

Do I need an account?

No. Obinot has no accounts. You only need an API key for the AI features. Here's how to get one.

Where are my notes stored?

On your phone only. Notes, audio, labels and settings never leave the device unless you export or share them.

Who sees my audio and text?

When you use AI, the audio or text goes directly from your phone to the provider you chose (Groq or Google Gemini) using your key. Obinot has no servers and doesn't see or relay it. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Recording, reading, searching, editing and organizing notes work offline, as do math formulas and diagrams. Transcription with AI, summaries and chat need an internet connection.

Which languages are supported?

The app is available in English, Spanish and German, with an in-app language picker (and per-app language on Android 13+). The AI can answer in 23 languages.

How do I install it? Is it on Google Play?

For now Obinot is distributed only through GitHub as an APK. The guide walks you through the install. It needs Android 7.0 or newer.

Is there an iPhone version?

No, Obinot is a native Android app.

What's the difference between Fast and Accurate mode?

Fast uses on-device transcription. Accurate records the full audio and has AI transcribe it when you open the note, which is usually more precise.

Is there a limit to the chat?

Each note keeps up to 40 messages. You can clear the conversation to start fresh; the note itself isn't affected.

How do I back up my notes?

Go to Settings → Data & System → Backup for a full backup (notes, audio, labels, preferences and chats). You can also export single notes or share them as Markdown.

What is the relationship with Binot?

Obinot is a community fork of Binot by DENSLnetion, updated for current AI models with a new design and features. It's a separate app, so both can coexist, and backups are compatible. Moving over?

I found a bug or have an idea

Open an issue on GitHub. Pull requests are welcome too.
